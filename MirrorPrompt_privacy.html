Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains how MirrorPrompt (the “App”) handles information when you use the iOS teleprompter application. In this Policy, “we,” “us,” and “our” refer to MirrorPrompt.

1. Information You Provide

The App may process scripts, titles, scene notes, imported documents, teleprompter preferences, and other content you choose to create or import. These materials are used to provide editing, prompting, and related features.

Where these features operate entirely on your device, their content is stored locally unless you choose to export or share it. We do not claim that all features operate exclusively offline; see the sections below.

2. Camera, Microphone, and Recordings

With your permission, the App may access your camera and microphone to record videos and audio. Recording content is processed for the features you select and may be saved on your device or exported at your direction. You can revoke camera and microphone access in iOS Settings.

The App uses camera and microphone access for recording features you choose to start. You can change these permissions in iOS Settings.

3. Speech Recognition and Voice Following

If you use voice-following or speech-recognition features, the App may process microphone audio and recognized text to follow your script. Depending on the recognition configuration and Apple services available on your device, audio or related data may be processed by Apple to provide speech recognition. On-device recognition is not guaranteed for every language, device, or configuration.

4. Floating Teleprompter and Picture in Picture

In-app floating prompting and any available system Picture in Picture features use your selected script and playback settings to display prompting content. Availability depends on the version of the App and iOS capabilities. Switching between these modes does not, by itself, require us to receive your script on a server.

5. Photos, Files, and Imports

When you choose to import files or select photos or videos, the App accesses the items you select to perform the requested operation. Exporting or sharing content may transfer it to destinations you choose, which are governed by those services' own policies.

6. Purchases and Subscriptions

Subscriptions and in-app purchases are processed by Apple through the App Store. We may receive transaction status or entitlement information needed to unlock paid features, but we do not receive your full payment card details from Apple.

7. Analytics, Diagnostics, and Third-Party Services

MirrorPrompt does not currently integrate third-party advertising, attribution, or analytics SDKs such as Firebase Analytics, Meta/Facebook SDK, or Adjust. We do not use third-party advertising trackers in the App. Apple and iOS may independently process information when you use App Store purchases, system services, or device diagnostics, according to their own policies. If our data practices change, we will update this policy.

8. Storage, Retention, and Deletion

Content stored locally remains on your device until you delete it through the App or iOS, subject to device backups and any copies you exported. Uninstalling the App generally removes its local app data, although iOS backups or external copies may remain. For any data processed by Apple or third parties, their retention practices may apply.

9. Data Sharing

We do not sell your scripts or recordings. Information may be shared with services you intentionally use (such as export destinations), Apple for purchases or speech recognition where applicable, and service providers disclosed in this Policy. We may disclose information where required by applicable law.

10. Security

We use reasonable safeguards appropriate to the App's architecture. No device, transmission, or storage method can be guaranteed completely secure. Protect your device and consider the sensitivity of content you import or record.

11. Children

The App is not specifically directed to children under 13. If you believe a child has provided personal information to us, contact us using the address below. Additional regional age requirements may apply.

12. International Users and Your Rights

Depending on your location and applicable law, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, or restrict certain personal information. For locally stored content, you can generally manage or delete it in the App or on your device. Contact us about information, if any, that we control outside your device.

13. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy to reflect changes to the App, services, or legal requirements. We will update the date above and provide additional notice where required.
